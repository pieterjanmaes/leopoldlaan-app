::note
These are pointers, not legal advice. Check the current rules with the Vlaamse overheid, your notary, architect or contractor.
::

## Regulations & administration

- **Asbestos inventory.** For buildings from before 2001, an asbestos inventory is needed before demolition or renovation works. It's already a seeded task — plan it early, because removal can change the order of the works.
- **EPC renovation obligation.** After buying a home with a poor EPC label, there's a deadline to reach a better label. Put it in the planning as a milestone and have a new EPC certificate drawn up at the end.
- **6% VAT.** Renovation of homes older than 10 years qualifies for 6% VAT. Give your contractor the certificate *before* they invoice; the app defaults quotes and invoices to 6%.
- **Electrical inspection (AREI).** Schedule it as soon as the wiring is done and before plastering, so corrections are still easy. It's a seeded milestone.
- **Permits.** Structural changes, façade changes or extensions may need a permit or notification (*omgevingsvergunning*). Check with the municipality before you plan the demolition.

## Money

- **Mijn VerbouwPremie.** Each premium has an application deadline after the final invoice. Fill in the *Premies* tab as soon as you know which works qualify, and keep the invoices and photos attached.
- **Keep a 10–15% buffer.** Older houses always hide surprises behind the walls. The contingency % in Instellingen adds it to the forecast.
- **Pay against progress.** Link invoices to approved quotes so the forecast doesn't count them twice, and pay advances per completed phase.

## Planning the works

- **Order matters.** Demolition → structure → roof & insulation → windows → rough electricity/plumbing/heating → plaster → screed → floors → paint → finishing. The seeded task chain follows this order; the planning warns when a task starts before the one it waits on.
- **Ventilation early.** If you insulate well and replace the windows, decide on ventilation (system C or D) before plastering — the ducts go in first.
- **Lead times.** Windows, kitchens and some tiles take weeks to deliver. Add an *order* task well before the *install* task and make the install wait on it.

## On site

- **Insurance.** Tell your home insurer about the works, and ask about site insurance (*ABR / werfverzekering*) and liability during demolition.
- **Photos before closing walls.** Photograph every wall and floor with pipes and cables before it's closed, and attach the photos to the room or a pin on the floor plan. You'll thank yourself when you drill a hole in five years.
- **Meter readings.** Note water, gas and electricity readings at the start and end of the works.
