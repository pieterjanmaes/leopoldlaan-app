## Quick wins

### 1. Make it a phone app (PWA)

A web app manifest and icons let you put the app on your home screen; it then opens full-screen like a native app. "Take a photo, attach it to a task" becomes two taps. Contractors will use the app on site, not at a desk, so this matters more than it seems.

*How:* `@vite-pwa/nuxt` (or a hand-written `manifest.webmanifest` + icons in `public/`). Start without offline caching — the data changes too often.

### 2. Decisions log

Record what was decided, when, by whom and why — e.g. *"Tiles: model X, colour Y, 60×60, ordered 12 Nov"*. It settles discussions later and saves the day when something must be re-ordered.

*How:* a `decisions` table (title, body, date, room, category, decided by) with Drive attachments for the sample photo or datasheet, shown on a new page and in the room's panel on the floor plan.

### 3. Shopping list & materials per room

What still needs buying: quantity, supplier, price, ordered / delivered. Delivered items can turn into an invoice with one click, and the total feeds the budget line.

## Bigger features

### 4. Weekly e-mail digest

Every Monday morning: this week's tasks, overdue items, invoices due soon and subsidy deadlines.

*How:* Cloudflare cron trigger + Nitro scheduled task + the Email binding, the same pattern as `boswachter-app` (`nitro.scheduledTasks` in `nuxt.config.ts`, `triggers.crons` in `wrangler.jsonc`).

### 5. AI invoice and quote scan

Upload a photo or PDF of an invoice; the app reads supplier, amounts excl./incl. VAT, VAT rate, invoice number and dates and opens a pre-filled *Factuur* form to check and save. Everything is already stored in structured form, so this is the most useful AI feature and a big time saver.

Other AI ideas once that works:

- a **chat assistant** over your own data ("How much is left for the bathroom?", "What's blocking the plasterwork?") using tool calls on the existing API;
- **plan generation**: describe the works and get a proposed task list with phases and dependencies.

### 6. Handover file (woningdossier)

When the works are done: warranties, manuals, the electrical inspection report, as-built plans, and where the stopcocks, meters and fuse box are. Link each item to a room and a pin on the floor plan so it's findable years later.

### 7. Before/after photos per room

A photo timeline per room. Nice to have, and useful when an insurer or a subsidy application asks for proof of the works.

### 8. Floor plan for externals

Let a contractor see the floor (read-only) with only the pins linked to their tasks. Requires extending `assertCanAccessFile` to the plan background and filtering markers by task visibility.
