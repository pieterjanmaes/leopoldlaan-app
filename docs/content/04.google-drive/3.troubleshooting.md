## "De Google Drive-koppeling is verlopen, koppel opnieuw"

Google refused to refresh the access token. Usual causes:

- the consent screen is still in **Testing** — refresh tokens die after 7 days. Publish it to *In production* and reconnect;
- access was revoked at [myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps](https://myaccount.google.com/connections);
- the password of the Google account changed.

Fix: Instellingen → **Opnieuw koppelen**.

## "Google gaf geen blijvende toegang"

Google only returns a refresh token the first time you consent. If the app was connected before and you connect again, Google may skip it. Remove the app under *Third-party apps* in your Google account and connect again.

## Error on the redirect back to the app

`redirect_uri_mismatch` means the URL in the Google OAuth client doesn't match exactly. It must be `https://<host>/auth/google-drive` — same scheme, host and port, no trailing slash.

## Thumbnails don't load

Google only makes thumbnails for images, PDFs and office files, and only some time after upload. The app falls back to an icon. Google-native Docs/Sheets have no downloadable content; they open in Drive.

## "Dit bestand staat niet in de projectmap"

A household member tried to open a file outside the project folder that isn't attached anywhere. Move the file into the project folder in Drive, or attach it from inside the folder.
