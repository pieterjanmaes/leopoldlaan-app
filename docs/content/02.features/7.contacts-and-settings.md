## Contacten

`/contacten` — contractors, suppliers, architect, notary… with company, trade, VAT number, phone and e-mail (click to call or mail). Each card shows how many open tasks are assigned to that contact. A contact is also what an external user is linked to.

## Instellingen

`/instellingen`, household only:

| Section | What |
|---|---|
| **Project** | Name (shown in the sidebar), address, start and target end date, contingency % |
| **Google Drive** | Connect / reconnect / disconnect, and switch the project folder by pasting a Drive folder link (owner only) |
| **Werkposten** | Categories with colour and icon; they colour the planning and split the budget |
| **Ruimtes** | Rooms with their floor |
| **Fases & mijlpalen** | Phases with dates; milestones are single-day markers |
| **Gebruikers** | Change roles, link externals to a contact, remove users, invite people (owner only) |

The arrows reorder catalog items; the whole list is renumbered so older items without a sort order line up too.

Rooms, categories, phases, contacts and user names are loaded once through `GET /api/lookups` and shared by every screen (`useLookups()`).

## Mijn account

`/account` — see your details and change your password. Changing it logs out your other devices.
