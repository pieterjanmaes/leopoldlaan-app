`/beslissingen` (`app/pages/beslissingen.vue`). Household only.

A renovation is a long series of choices — heating, ventilation, tiles, the kitchen — and months later nobody remembers why something was chosen or which exact product it was. The decisions log keeps both the **open questions** and the **answers**.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Te beslissen** | Still open, optionally with a *beslissen vóór* deadline. Overdue deadlines turn red |
| **Beslist** | Settled. The app stamps *beslist op* (today, unless you set a date) and *beslist door* (you) |
| **Vervangen** | Overruled by a later decision, kept for the history |

Reopening a decision clears the date and the person. The logic is in `decisionStamps()` (`server/utils/decisions.ts`) and unit-tested.

## Fields

- **Wat** — the question or the choice, e.g. *Vloerbekleding gelijkvloers*.
- **Opties & overwegingen / Wat is er beslist, en waarom** — free text; the label follows the status.
- **Referentie** — product, model, colour code, sizes: everything needed to order it again. Shown on the card and searchable.
- **Leverancier / aannemer**, **ruimte**, **werkpost** and a **gekoppelde taak**.
- **Stalen, foto's & fiches** — Drive attachments: a photo of the sample, the datasheet, the quote.

## Where decisions show up

- **Overzicht** — a *Nog te beslissen* card with the open decisions, soonest deadline first.
- **Grondplan** — selecting a zone or pin linked to a room lists that room's decisions.
- **Search (⌘K)** — titles and references, linking to `/beslissingen?beslissing=<id>`, which opens it.
- **Phone** — in the bottom bar, between Taken and Grondplan.

## Seeded questions

A fresh seed adds five open decisions with deadlines before the phase that needs them: heating type, ventilation system C or D, sockets and lights per room, floor finish (it determines the screed thickness) and the kitchen.
