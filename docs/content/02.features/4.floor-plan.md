`/grondplan` (`app/pages/grondplan.vue`, `app/components/CFloorPlanCanvas.vue`). Household only.

## Floors

Each tab is a floor (`floor_plans`). On an empty page, **Verdiepingen uit de ruimtes** creates one floor per distinct `floor` value of your rooms.

A floor can have a **background image** from Drive (pick or upload): a PNG/JPG/SVG export from a drawing tool such as [openPlan3D](https://github.com/laanlabs/openPlan3D), or a photo/scan of the architect's plan. PDFs can't be drawn on directly — export a page as an image first. Without an image you draw on a grid.

## Marking up

| Tool | How |
|---|---|
| **Selecteer** | Click a zone or pin to edit it; drag the background to pan, drag pins to move them |
| **Zone** | Click the corners; close by clicking the first point, double-clicking or pressing Enter. Esc cancels |
| **Pin** | Click where it should go; pins are numbered in creation order |

Scroll to zoom (0.5×–6×); the buttons in the corner zoom and reset the view.

Each marker has a **title**, **colour**, **room**, **linked task** and **note**. A zone without a title shows its room's name; a zone linked to a room lists that room's open tasks in the panel.

## How it's stored

`plan_markers.points` is a JSON array of `[x, y]` **fractions of the plan** (0..1), not pixels. Markers therefore stay in place at any screen size and when the background image is replaced by a sharper version. The floor stores the image's aspect ratio; when a different image loads, the canvas reports the new ratio and it's saved.

## Implementation notes

- Rendering uses [`vue-konva`](https://konvajs.org/docs/vue/), registered in `app/plugins/vueKonva.client.ts`. Konva needs a real `<canvas>`, so `CFloorPlanCanvas` is always wrapped in `<ClientOnly>`.
- Konva needs real colour values, not Tailwind classes — `app/utils/colors.ts` maps each category colour to its hex value.
- The background is loaded through the Drive proxy (`/api/drive/files/:id/content`), so the same [access checks](/google-drive/how-it-works) apply.
