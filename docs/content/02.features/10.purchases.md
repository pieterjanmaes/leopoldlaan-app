`/aankopen` (`app/pages/aankopen.vue`). Household only, and in the phone's bottom bar — it's the list you take to the store.

## Adding

Type in the quick-add field the way you'd write a list and press Enter:

| You type | Becomes |
|---|---|
| `12 m² vloertegels` | 12 m² · vloertegels |
| `3 zakken tegellijm` | 3 zak · tegellijm |
| `2,5 l primer` | 2.5 l · primer |
| `4 stopcontacten` | 4 st · stopcontacten |
| `silicone transparant` | 1 st · silicone transparant |

The parser is `parseQuickMaterial()` in `shared/utils/materials.ts` (unit-tested). Click an item to add the rest: price per unit (incl. VAT), when it's needed on site, store or supplier, room, category, the **task** it's for, the **decision** it belongs to (which fills in room, category and supplier), a product link and notes.

## Statuses

**Te kopen → Besteld → Geleverd.** The round button in front of each item moves it one step and stamps *besteld op* / *geleverd op*; moving back clears them.

## Ordering in time

An item is needed on site on its own date, or — if empty — on the **start date of the linked task**. When something isn't ordered yet and is needed within **14 days** (`ORDER_WARNING_DAYS`) or is already late, it shows in red, in an alert at the top of the page, and in a *Te bestellen* card on the overview.

Link long-lead items (windows, kitchen, tiles) to their task right away, so the warning comes in time.

## Grouping

Per ruimte (default), per winkel (handy in the store) or per werkpost, each group with its total.

## From receipt to invoice

Tick the items that are on one receipt or invoice and click **Maak factuur**. The app creates one invoice under *Budget → Facturen*:

- the total is the sum of the items, at **21% VAT** by default (store-bought materials; 6% only applies when a contractor supplies and installs);
- store, category and room are filled in when all items share them;
- the item list goes into the notes;
- the items are marked delivered and linked to the invoice, so they can't be booked twice.

## In the budget

Items with a price that aren't on an invoice yet count as **expected cost** for their category (`purchasesCents` in `summarizeBudget`). Once they're on an invoice, they count as that invoice instead.

## On the floor plan

Selecting a zone or pin linked to a room shows a button with that room's open purchases, linking to `/aankopen?ruimte=<id>`.
