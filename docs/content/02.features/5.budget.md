`/budget` (`app/pages/budget.vue`, data from `GET /api/budget`). Household only. All amounts are stored as **integer cents**.

## Tabs

| Tab | What |
|---|---|
| **Overzicht** | Bars per werkpost: paid (solid), invoiced or approved (light), rest of the plan (track); red when the forecast exceeds the plan. Below: the net final cost |
| **Budget** | Your own estimates (`budget_lines`), per werkpost and optionally per room |
| **Offertes** | Quotes with contractor, amount excl. VAT, VAT rate and status (*aangevraagd*, *ontvangen*, *goedgekeurd*, *afgewezen*) |
| **Facturen** | Invoices and other expenses: amounts, invoice number, dates, paid on, payment method. Exportable to Excel |
| **Premies** | Subsidies with expected and received amount, status and application deadline |

Quotes and invoices have attachments (the PDF from the contractor, in Drive).

## VAT

Rates are 0, 6, 12 or 21% (default **6%**, the renovation rate for homes older than 10 years). For quotes the total incl. VAT is computed (`shared/utils/vat.ts`). For invoices it's computed too, but stored and editable: invoices round per line, so the total on paper is what counts.

## The forecast

Per werkpost (`summarizeBudget` in `shared/utils/budget.ts`):

```
known    = all invoices
         + (approved quotes − invoices already linked to a quote)
         + purchases with a price that aren't on an invoice yet
forecast = max(known, planned)
```

So the plan counts until real numbers exceed it, an invoice linked to an approved quote isn't counted twice, and a purchase stops counting as expected once it's [booked on an invoice](/features/purchases).

Totals (`budgetTotals`):

```
contingency  = planned × contingency %        (Instellingen → Project, default 10%)
subsidies    = expected (or received, if higher) of subsidies that weren't rejected
net forecast = forecast + contingency − subsidies
```

Both functions are pure and unit-tested in `test/unit/budget.test.ts`.
